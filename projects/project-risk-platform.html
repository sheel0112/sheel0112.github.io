Digital Risk Platform – Multi-Sport Pricing & Exposure System

Risk Department Product Owner · Angstrom Sports / Entain · 2024–2026

What I did: Took the company's first in-house risk platform from vision to production, and built the first cross-functional product team (10+ engineers, data and design) to deliver it.

Result: Replaced a third-party provider, saving $4m a year. Bringing AI-assisted development into the team lifted front-end delivery by 20%.

Summary

A full modernisation of Angstrom’s risk architecture, unifying pricing, exposure, liquidity, and trader controls into one cross-sport platform used across NBA, NFL, and MLB.

Context

Angstrom operated multiple fragmented tools across sports. Traders lacked consistent controls, visibility, and reliable workflows, creating operational bottlenecks during high-pressure events.

Problem

Pricing systems were fast, but risk controls were siloed. Teams needed unified visibility, actionable guardrails, and automation to reduce manual workload and improve reaction speed.

Constraints & Stakes

  • High-risk, real-money environment where seconds matter
  • Cross-team dependencies: FE, BE, modelling, trading, data
  • Hard seasonal deadlines (NBA/NFL kick-offs)
  • Complex distributed architecture (Kafka streams, microservices)

My Role

As Product Owner for the entire digital risk platform, I owned roadmap definition, cross-sport alignment, prototyping, delivery leadership, and stakeholder communication across Entain, BetMGM, and internal teams.

Actions

  1. Discovery: Interviewed 30+ traders to map workflows & pain points.
  2. Architecture: Designed a unified risk schema across exposure, limits, automation, and pricing impact.
  3. Design: Introduced AI-assisted development and Figma → React prototyping, increasing front-end delivery by 20%.
  4. Team formation: Built the first cross-functional Risk Product Team.
  5. KPI Framework: Defined adoption, latency, exposure accuracy & automation metrics.
  6. Rollout: Coordinated multi-market onboarding across NBA, NFL & MLB trading teams.

Impact

  • 20% increase in front-end delivery through AI-assisted development.
  • Unified risk behaviour across all major US sports.
  • Created the foundation for a proprietary feature and future automation pillars.

Where this applies

The problem underneath is common to any business that takes on risk in real time: see exposure as it builds, act on it within agreed limits, and be able to show what happened afterwards.

  • Real-time exposure and limits: trading desks, payments and fraud, lending, insurance accumulation
  • Automated decisions with human override: pricing engines, credit decisioning, dynamic pricing in travel and e-commerce
  • Audit trails and tiered permissions: any regulated product, from financial services to healthcare
  • Replacing a vendor with in-house capability: build-versus-buy calls in any platform team
